**ADVANCED CINEMATIC LANGUAGE**

Veo 3 responds well to professional filmmaking terminology. Use these terms to achieve specific visual effects:

**Shot Types:**
- **Establishing shot** - Wide view showing location/context
- **Wide shot / Long shot** - Shows full subject with surroundings
- **Medium shot** - Waist up, conversational framing
- **Close-up** - Tight on face or object, emphasizes detail
- **Extreme close-up** - Eyes only, or product detail
- **Over-the-shoulder** - View from behind character's shoulder
- **Point-of-view (POV)** - Camera as character's eyes
- **Dutch angle / Canted angle** - Tilted frame for unease

**Camera Movements:**
- **Dolly in/out** - Camera moves toward/away from subject on tracks
- **Tracking shot** - Camera follows subject's movement smoothly
- **Pan** - Camera rotates horizontally (left/right)
- **Tilt** - Camera rotates vertically (up/down)
- **Crane/Boom** - Camera rises or descends dramatically
- **Steadicam** - Smooth handheld-style movement
- **Handheld** - Intentional camera shake for realism/urgency
- **Whip pan** - Extremely fast pan creating blur between scenes
- **Orbit/Arc shot** - Camera circles around subject

**Advanced Editing Techniques:**
- **Match cut** - Visual similarity connects two shots (e.g., shape match)
- **Jump cut** - Jarring time jump in same scene (e.g., person in different positions)
- **Smash cut** - Abrupt transition to contrasting scene
- **Slow motion** - Action slowed for emphasis or beauty
- **Time-lapse** - Accelerated passage of time
- **Freeze frame** - Motion stops on single frame
- **Split diopter** - Two depth planes in focus simultaneously

**Lighting Terms:**
- **Golden hour** - Warm, soft light at sunrise/sunset
- **Blue hour** - Cool twilight after sunset
- **High-key lighting** - Bright, minimal shadows, upbeat mood
- **Low-key lighting** - Strong shadows, dramatic contrast, moody
- **Rembrandt lighting** - Triangle of light on cheek, cinematic portrait
- **Backlighting** - Light from behind subject, creates silhouette or rim light
- **Practical lighting** - Visible light sources in scene (lamps, windows, neon)
- **Volumetric lighting** - Light beams visible through fog/dust

**Compositional Techniques:**
- **Rule of thirds** - Subject positioned at intersection of 9-grid
- **Leading lines** - Lines guide eye toward subject
- **Frame within frame** - Doorway, window, or arch frames subject
- **Symmetry** - Balanced, centered composition
- **Depth layering** - Foreground, midground, background elements
- **Negative space** - Empty space around subject for emphasis
- **Bokeh** - Blurred background with aesthetic quality

**Usage Examples:**
- "Close-up shot with shallow depth of field, bokeh background, Rembrandt lighting on subject's face"
- "Tracking shot following character through crowded street, handheld camera for gritty realism"
- "Match cut from spinning vinyl record to overhead shot of spinning ceiling fan"
- "Dutch angle, low-key lighting, extreme close-up on character's eyes showing fear"
- "Slow dolly out combined with slow motion, golden hour backlighting, subject silhouetted"
