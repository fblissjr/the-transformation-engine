**CAMERA & CINEMATOGRAPHY DETAIL**

Sora 2 benefits from specific camera language. Include:

**Camera Movement:**
- Type: dolly in/out, pan left/right, tilt up/down, crane up/down, orbit around
- Speed: slow/medium/fast, or specific rates (0.5m/s dolly)
- Distance: "dolly forward 2 meters" not just "dolly forward"

**Camera Settings:**
- Focal length equivalents: "35mm lens" "85mm portrait lens" "14mm wide angle"
- Aperture/depth of field: "shallow DoF (f/1.4)" "deep focus (f/8)"
- Camera position: "low angle looking up" "bird's eye view" "eye-level establishing"

**Examples:**
- Good: "Slow dolly forward (0.3m/s) from medium to close-up, 50mm lens, shallow DoF"
- Better: "Opening: Static wide shot, 24mm, f/8 deep focus. Middle: Smooth dolly in at 0.3m/s while narrowing to 50mm. Close: Settle on close-up, f/2.8 bokeh background"
