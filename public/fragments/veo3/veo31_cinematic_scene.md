# Cinematic Realism Scene Template (Veo 3.1)

## Scene Type Characteristics
- Wide/establishing/aerial shots (environment-focused)
- Dynamic camera movement (following, tracking, panning)
- Photorealistic rendering
- Strong cinematography (lighting, composition, atmosphere)
- Environmental focus over character dialogue
- Minimal or no dialogue

## Optimal Prompt Structure

```
[Camera type + movement] [subject + action] [location/environment], [time of day/lighting].
[Visual details]. [Audio cues - sound effects].
```

## Camera Keywords (Essential for Cinematic Scenes)

**Shot Types**:
- Drone shot, aerial view, establishing shot, tracking shot
- Dolly shot, crane shot, panning shot, orbiting shot

**Camera Movement**:
- Following, panning, orbiting, descending, ascending
- Pulling back, pushing in, sweeping across
- Slow tracking, smooth glide, dynamic pursuit

**Framing**:
- Wide shot, ultra-wide, extreme wide angle
- Establishing shot, landscape orientation
- Environmental perspective

## Visual & Lighting Guidance

**Cinematography**:
- Specify time of day (golden hour, sunset, dawn, midday)
- Natural lighting conditions (harsh sun, soft diffused light, dramatic shadows)
- Weather/atmosphere (misty, clear, overcast, hazy)

**Composition**:
- Leading lines (winding roads, rivers, pathways)
- Depth elements (foreground, midground, background layers)
- Rule of thirds, symmetry, geometric patterns

## Optimal Length
**150-250 words** - Camera-focused and concise

## Official Example (from Google Docs)

```
Drone shot following a classic red convertible driven by a man along a winding
coastal road at sunset, waves crashing against the rocks below.
The convertible accelerates fast and the engine roars loudly.
```

## Advanced Example (Detailed)

```
Aerial drone shot descending from a mountain peak toward a vast canyon at golden hour.
The camera follows a winding river that cuts through red rock formations, casting long
shadows across the valley floor. A lone eagle soars through the frame, wings catching
the warm sunset light.

The river rushes over smooth stones, a distant waterfall echoes through the canyon,
gentle wind rustles sparse desert vegetation.

The shot slowly pulls back to reveal the full majesty of the landscape as the sun
touches the horizon, painting the sky in vibrant oranges and purples.
```

## Key Success Factors
1. Specific camera type and movement
2. Clear subject and action
3. Environmental context and lighting
4. Photorealistic visual details
5. Audio design (natural sounds, ambient environment)
6. Cinematic framing and composition
