# Dialogue & Sound Effects Scene Template (Veo 3.1)

## Scene Type Characteristics
- Close-up to medium shots (character-focused framing)
- Explicit dialogue in quotes with delivery styles
- Detailed sound effects (SFX) layering
- Ambient noise descriptions
- Character interactions and body language

## Optimal Prompt Structure

```
[Shot type + framing] of [subject] [action], [environment details].
[Character 1]: [Delivery style] "[Quoted dialogue]"
[Character 2]: [Delivery style] "[Quoted dialogue]"
[Sound effects], [ambient noise]
```

## Audio Guidance (CRITICAL for Veo 3.1)

**Dialogue**:
- Use quotes for all speech
- Specify delivery style: murmurs, whispers, shouts, excitedly, calmly states, voice tight with fear
- Include character actions during dialogue: looks at him, staring at wall, hand on knife

**Sound Effects (SFX)**:
- Explicit > Implicit
- Layer primary, secondary, and ambient sounds
- Examples: torchlight flickering, footsteps on damp earth, tires screeching, engine roaring

**Ambiance**:
- Environmental soundscape descriptions
- Examples: lone bird chirps, distant traffic, eerie hum, wind rustling leaves, waves crashing

## Optimal Length
**200-300 words** for dialogue-heavy scenes

## Official Example (from Google Docs)

```
A close up of two people staring at a cryptic drawing on a wall, torchlight flickering.
A man murmurs, 'This must be it. That's the secret code.'
The woman looks at him and whispering excitedly, 'What did you find?'
```

## Advanced Example (Detailed)

```
A wide shot of a misty Pacific Northwest forest. Two exhausted hikers, a man and a
woman, push through ferns when the man stops abruptly, staring at a tree. Close-up:
Fresh, deep claw marks are gouged into the tree's bark.

Man: (Hand on his hunting knife) "That's no ordinary bear."
Woman: (Voice tight with fear, scanning the woods) "Then what is it?"

A rough bark, snapping twigs, footsteps on the damp earth. A lone bird chirps.
```

## Key Success Factors
1. Quoted dialogue with delivery cues
2. Character body language and expressions
3. Explicit SFX descriptions (not implied)
4. Layered ambient sounds
5. Close framing that captures facial expressions
