**VEO 3 EXAMPLE: Portrait Comes to Life**

**Technical Specs:** Duration: 8s | Resolution: 720p | Aspect Ratio: 16:9 | Frame Rate: 24fps
**Input Type:** Image-to-Video (starting frame provided)

**STARTING IMAGE DESCRIPTION (Reference Frame):**
Professional portrait photograph. Woman in her 30s, direct camera gaze, centered composition, shoulders and head visible (mid-chest framing). Dark curly hair frames face, warm brown eyes, neutral expression (not smiling, not frowning - contemplative). Wearing cream-colored knit sweater, simple silver necklace. Background is soft-focus home interior - warm earth tones, bookshelves barely visible in bokeh, golden late afternoon light from window (not visible but implied by lighting direction). Shallow depth of field (f/2.8 equivalent). Color grading warm and inviting (3200K). Image feels like intimate portrait session, not candid snapshot. High resolution, sharp focus on eyes, professional photography quality.

**Context (Continuous from Starting Image):**
Home study or reading room. Subject sits in comfortable armchair (not visible in frame, suggested by her position and angle). Window to camera-left provides natural light (warm late afternoon sun). Background bookshelves contain mixture of books and small objects (picture frames, plant, ceramic mug on shelf). Cozy intellectual domestic space. Time: approximately 4:30 PM on autumn afternoon.

**Action (Evolution from Static Image):**
0-1s: Complete stillness - matches starting image exactly, no movement (establishes continuity)
1-2.5s: First signs of life - subject blinks slowly (natural, not exaggerated), begins subtle breathing movement (chest and shoulders rising gently)
2.5-4s: Eyes shift slightly, looking just past camera (as if someone entered room), micro-expression begins forming (hint of recognition)
4-5.5s: Smile gradually develops - starts at eyes (crinkling at corners), spreads to mouth (closed-mouth smile, genuine warmth), head tilts very slightly (3-4 degrees to right, subtle welcoming gesture)
5.5-7s: She inhales as if about to speak, lips part slightly, smile broadens showing hint of teeth, eyes brighten with engagement
7-8s: Begins to lean forward very subtly (2-3cm forward), as if responding to off-camera person, maintains eye contact direction (now looking slightly right of camera)

Transformation is GRADUAL and SUBTLE - not dramatic animation, but natural transition from still portrait to living person. Movement constrained and realistic - matches starting image's intimate portraiture style.

**Style:**
Photographic realism matching starting image aesthetic. Cinematic portrait photography style. Maintaining original image's professional quality, lighting consistency, and color grading. Style references: Annie Leibovitz intimate portraits, Gregory Crewdson staged realism, film photography aesthetic (gentle grain, organic color response). No artificial effects - purely organic human movement.

**Camera Motion:**
Near-static with extremely subtle movement. First 3 seconds: completely locked off (matches starting image framing). Seconds 3-6: imperceptibly slow dolly-in (approximately 3-4cm forward movement over 3 seconds, barely noticeable but adds intimacy). Seconds 6-8: continues slow dolly-in (additional 3cm). Total movement: 6-7cm over 5 seconds - creates subtle growing connection without disrupting portrait framing. Camera remains at eye-level throughout. This minimal movement preserves starting image's composition while adding cinematic dimension.

**Audio Elements (Critical for Image-to-Video):**

**Rationale:** Starting image is silent (photograph), so audio DEFINES the transition to video. Sound must emerge naturally, not jarring.

**Audio Introduction (0-2s):**
- Begins with near-silence (respects stillness of starting image)
- At 1-second mark: first sound - very quiet breath (exhale through nose, subtle, human)
- Gentle room ambiance fades in gradually: subtle environmental room tone (presence of space, not specific sounds yet)

**Ambient Soundscape (2-8s):**
- **Primary ambient layer:** Quiet room tone - subtle air movement, barely audible, creates sense of interior space
- **Background detail:** Clock ticking somewhere in room (not visible, but heard faintly, reinforces domestic setting, rhythmic, warm sound)
- **Distant layer:** Muffled sounds from elsewhere in house - very faint, indistinct, suggests lived-in home (perhaps distant kettle whistle at 5s mark, very quiet)
- **Natural sounds:** Fabric of sweater rustling softly as she shifts (synchronized to 7s forward lean)

**Music (Non-Diegetic, Emergence):**
Extremely subtle acoustic guitar fingerpicking - begins at 3-second mark, very quiet (20% volume), warm intimate tone, no rhythm or melody - just gentle arpeggiated notes, organic and hesitant, matches emotional shift from stillness to engagement. Sounds like someone in adjacent room playing guitar quietly. Could also be interpreted as diegetic (someone off-screen). Intentionally ambiguous.

**Voice/Breath:**
- At 5.5s (synchronized to inhale): audible breath intake (natural, preparing to speak)
- At 7.5s: Very beginning of spoken word starts - just the initial phoneme "H" sound (as if saying "Hi" or "Hey"), but video ends before word completes. This creates anticipation and natural end point. Her voice quality: warm, medium pitch, intimate volume (as if speaking to someone nearby).

**Sound Design Philosophy:**
Sound emerges from silence gradually, mirrors visual transformation from still to motion. No jarring transitions. Every sound motivated by visible action or implied domestic setting. Maintains intimate scale matching portrait framing - no loud or dramatic sounds. Audio completes what starting image cannot provide: the dimension of time and life.

**Lighting & Mood (Maintaining Image Consistency):**
Lighting matches starting image exactly (continuity is critical for image-to-video):
- Warm natural light from camera-left (late afternoon sun quality, 3200K)
- Soft Rembrandt-style lighting on face (triangle of light on shadow side of cheek)
- Catchlights in eyes (from window, maintains throughout)
- Background remains consistent soft bokeh in warm tones
- No lighting changes during 8 seconds - sun position doesn't shift perceptibly
- Mood: Maintains starting image's intimate warmth, adds layer of engagement and emerging connection as she responds to off-screen presence

**Background/Setting (Consistency with Image):**
Background remains exactly as starting image throughout:
- Soft-focus bookshelves (no sharp details, preserves shallow depth of field)
- Warm earth tone colors (browns, creams, muted oranges from book spines)
- No background movement (books don't shift, no people walking past)
- Bokeh circles from window light reflecting off objects maintain position
- Setting feels continuous and stable - all change is in subject, not environment

**Composition (Preserving Starting Image Frame):**
Starting composition maintained with minimal adjustment:
- Subject centered (matching starting image)
- Headroom consistent (top of head approximately 10% from top edge)
- Eye-line at upper third (traditional portrait composition)
- Shoulders frame bottom of shot naturally
- Negative space balanced left and right of subject
- As subject leans forward slightly (7-8s), camera's subtle dolly-in compensates to maintain framing scale
- Composition feels stable despite subtle movement - honors original portrait while adding life

**Character Notes (Bringing Still Image to Life):**
Transformation demonstrates:
- **Micro-expressions:** Subtle before obvious - eyes change before mouth, realism in progression
- **Natural timing:** Blink rate normal (every 3-5 seconds), movements don't feel rushed
- **Authentic response:** She's reacting to something off-screen (visitor entering room, partner calling her name) - gives purpose to transformation
- **Restrained performance:** Not theatrical - this is real human movement, not acted animation
- **Emotional arc:** Neutral contemplation → recognition → warm welcoming - clear but subtle
- **Body language:** Forward lean signals engagement, openness, welcoming energy toward off-screen person

**Image-to-Video Specific Techniques:**

**1. Continuity is Sacred:**
- First 1 second MUST match starting image exactly (pose, expression, lighting, framing)
- Any deviation breaks illusion that video continues from still image
- Lighting consistency maintained throughout entire 8 seconds

**2. Gradual Animation:**
- Movement emerges slowly, respecting stillness of starting image
- Sequence: breath → blink → eye movement → expression → larger motion
- Never jump from still to dynamic - ease into motion

**3. Motivated Change:**
- Give reason for movement (she's responding to off-camera stimulus)
- Without motivation, animation feels arbitrary or puppet-like
- Off-screen presence (unseen person) provides narrative justification

**4. Technical Constraints:**
- Maintain starting image's depth of field (don't suddenly shift to deep focus)
- Preserve color grading and exposure (no dramatic shifts)
- Keep camera movement minimal (prevents disorienting viewer)
- Background should remain relatively static (focus change is in subject)

**5. Audio Crucial for Transition:**
- Photograph is silent; video needs sound to feel real
- Audio emergence parallels visual animation (from stillness to life)
- Diegetic sounds anchor scene in reality (breath, fabric, room tone)
- Music optional but adds emotional dimension

**Why This Works:**
- Respects starting image composition and aesthetic (continuity maintained)
- Gradual natural movement (not jarring animation)
- Clear narrative motivation (responding to off-screen presence)
- Audio thoughtfully introduced (parallels visual transformation)
- Subtle camera movement enhances without disrupting (minimal dolly-in)
- Realistic human behavior (blink rate, micro-expressions, timing)
- Emotional arc clear but restrained (contemplative to welcoming)
- 8 seconds provides complete transformation without feeling rushed
- Technical continuity (lighting, color, focus) preserved throughout

**Demonstrates:**
- Image-to-video transition best practices (1-second exact match, gradual animation)
- Motivated movement vs arbitrary animation
- Audio introduction strategy for still-to-motion (silence to subtle presence)
- Maintaining photographic aesthetic in video format

- Subtle camera movement in constrained framing (minimal dolly-in)
- Off-screen presence as narrative device (unseen character motivates response)
- Continuity maintenance (lighting, color, focus consistency)
- Natural human timing (blink rate, breath, movement speed)
- Intimate portrait scale preserved (no dramatic shifts)

**Image-to-Video Prompting Template:**

When using image uploads with Veo 3, structure prompt as:
1. **Describe starting image in detail** (establishes continuity expectation)
2. **Specify how image should evolve** (action progression)
3. **Maintain consistency cues** (lighting, framing, style stay constant)
4. **Motivate the change** (why does stillness become motion?)
5. **Add audio dimension** (sound that wasn't present in still image)
6. **Describe camera behavior** (usually minimal for portraits)

This example demonstrates all six elements for successful image-to-video generation with Veo 3.
