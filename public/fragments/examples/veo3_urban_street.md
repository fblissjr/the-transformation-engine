**Style:**
Documentary realism with observational cinema aesthetic. Reference: Frederick Wiseman urban observation, Safdie Brothers street photography influence. Natural lighting, authentic moments, no artifice. Handheld camera suggesting documentary crew capturing real life. Slight grain texture (like 16mm film stock) adding gritty authenticity. Color palette: Urban neutrals - greys, browns, muted greens, with accent of yellow (taxi). Depth of field showing real-world camera behavior - subject in focus, background softly blurred but still readable. Style emphasizes: authenticity over beauty, moment over composition, real over idealized.

**Camera Motion:**
Handheld documentary style with subtle natural movement. Camera positioned at eye level (approximately 5'8" height) about 6 feet from subject, slightly off-center right. Movement characteristics: Gentle organic float and micro-adjustments suggesting real camera operator breathing and shifting weight (not locked-off tripod). Movement is NOT smooth cinema tracking - has slight hesitation and organic feel of documentary cinematographer responding to subject's motion. Camera remains respectful distance (not invasive close-up) maintaining observational aesthetic.

Sound design emphasizes: urban density, everyday mundane reality, public space ambient, rhythmic city pulse.
