**VEO 3 EXAMPLE: Tech Product Reveal**

**Technical Specs:** Duration: 8s | Resolution: 720p | Aspect Ratio: 16:9 | Frame Rate: 24fps

**Subject:**
Sleek wireless earbuds, matte black finish with subtle metallic accents, sitting on a minimalist white pedestal. Product approximately 2 inches tall, case slightly open revealing the earbuds inside with soft LED glow indicators (small blue lights). High-end tech aesthetic, premium materials visible in close detail.

**Context:**
Studio environment with pure white cyclorama backdrop creating infinite horizon effect. Pedestal is simple geometric form - white cylindrical base 6 inches in diameter. Lighting creates clean shadows emphasizing product form. Environment suggests high-end tech commercial aesthetic - pristine, modern, sophisticated.

**Action:**
Product remains stationary on pedestal. Camera executes smooth orbital movement. At second 3, the earbud case lid slowly closes with precise mechanical motion (subtle click sound). At second 6, a gentle pulsing of the blue LED indicators suggests active pairing mode. Product stays center frame throughout entire movement.

**Style:**
Premium commercial product photography aesthetic. Hyper-realistic rendering with attention to material properties - matte surfaces, metallic accents, soft LED glow. Clean, minimalist, Apple-style commercial look. High production value with precise lighting control.

**Camera Motion:**
Smooth 180-degree orbit shot around product at constant height (eye-level with product, approximately 8 inches above pedestal surface). Camera starts at front-right position, moves counter-clockwise in perfect circular arc at radius of 18 inches, ending at front-left position. Movement is slow, steady, and perfectly smooth (no handheld shake). Arc completes in exactly 8 seconds - approximately 22.5 degrees per second rotation rate. Maintains consistent framing with product centered.

**Audio Elements:**
Professional voiceover (male voice, confident and warm, mid-30s vocal quality): "Sound. Redefined." Voiceover begins at second 2, phrase completes by second 4. Background: Subtle ambient electronic soundscape - soft synth pad creating futuristic atmosphere, very low in mix. At second 3 when case closes: precise mechanical "click" sound effect (satisfying, premium quality). At second 6 when LEDs pulse: soft electronic "pulse" tone synchronized with visual. Overall audio mix: 70% voiceover, 20% ambient music, 10% sound effects. All audio pristine quality, broadcast-standard.

**Lighting & Mood:**
Three-point lighting setup executed with precision. Key light: Large softbox from camera-left creating main illumination and subtle gradient across product. Fill light: Diffused panel from camera-right preventing harsh shadows. Rim light: Focused spotlight from behind-right creating edge definition and metallic highlights. Color temperature: Cool white (5500K) maintaining tech product aesthetic. Mood: Premium, sophisticated, aspirational - the feeling of luxury technology. Shadows are soft but present, adding depth without drama.

**Background/Setting:**
Pure white infinity backdrop with subtle gradient - lighter at horizon line, slightly darker at top and bottom of frame. No visible horizon seam (classic seamless backdrop technique). Extremely minimal - all focus directed to product. Slight vignetting in post-processing darkens frame edges subtly, keeping eye centered on product. Background remains consistent throughout camera orbit.

**Composition:**
Product centered in frame throughout entire shot using Dutch composition principles adapted for commercial work. Vertical center axis maintained as camera orbits. Foreground: Pedestal edge visible at bottom third of frame providing context and scale. Midground: Product occupies central third of frame vertically, slightly lower than mathematical center (following rule of asymmetrical balance). Background: Clean white infinity taking up top two-thirds. Negative space used strategically - product has breathing room emphasizing its premium positioning. As camera orbits, changing angle reveals different product facets while maintaining compositional balance.

**Technical Notes for Veo 3:**
- Voiceover timing is critical: "Sound" at 2.0s, "Redefined" at 3.2s with natural pause between
- Camera orbit must be perfectly smooth (no acceleration/deceleration artifacts)
- Product lighting must maintain consistency as camera angle changes
- LED pulse animation synchronized with audio cue at exactly 6.0s
- Case-closing mechanical motion at 3.0s must feel weighty and precise (premium tactile quality)

This example demonstrates: Product-focused narrative with precise timing, voiceover + ambient audio + sound effects layering, complex camera motion with specific parameters, premium commercial aesthetic, attention to audio-visual synchronization, and technical execution details that leverage Veo 3's native audio and high-fidelity visual capabilities.
