# Lighting Motivation (GPT-5 Stage-1)

## Required Lighting Elements

Every lighting setup must include:
1. **Key light source** - Primary light motivation
2. **Direction** - Position relative to camera (left, right, above, behind)
3. **Color temperature** - Kelvin value (2800K-6500K)
4. **Key/fill ratio** - Contrast ratio (1.5:1, 2:1, 3:1)
5. **Rim/back light** (optional) - Separation from background
6. **Grading/aesthetic** - Color treatment, mood

## Motivated Lighting (Critical)

All light sources must be **motivated** by the scene environment:

**Natural sources**:
- "Soft 5600K key from camera right motivated by late-afternoon sun"
- "Morning sun back rim at 5600K"
- "Cool 5600K daylight from window right"

**Practical sources**:
- "Warm tungsten key 3000K from table lamp"
- "Key 3200K from camera left motivated by fireplace"
- "Soft key from window bounce (5600K)"

**Artificial sources**:
- "Soft key from neon signs (6000K)"
- "Key spotlight 3200K from camera left"
- "Key 5000K from ceiling panels"

## Color Temperature Guide

**Warm (2800K-3400K)**:
- Tungsten bulbs: 2800-3000K
- Fireplace/candles: 2800-3200K
- Indoor lamps: 3000-3400K
- Use for: Cozy, intimate, nostalgic moods

**Neutral (4200K-5600K)**:
- Daylight balanced: 5200-5600K
- Overcast sky: 6000-6500K
- Studio lighting: 5000-5200K
- Use for: Natural, balanced, realistic scenes

**Cool (6000K-7000K)**:
- Blue hour: 6500-7000K
- Neon signs: 6000-6500K
- Fluorescent: 4200-5000K
- Use for: Night scenes, urban, sci-fi

## Key/Fill Ratios

**1.5:1 (Soft, even lighting)**:
- Natural outdoor scenes
- Bright, airy interiors
- Minimal drama

**2:1 (Standard cinematic)**:
- Most narrative scenes
- Balanced contrast
- Professional look

**2.5:1 (Moderate drama)**:
- Fashion, lifestyle
- Controlled contrast
- Defined shadows

**3:1 (High drama)**:
- Product shots
- Theatrical lighting
- Strong shadows

## Rim/Back Light

Add rim light for **subject separation**:
- "Add subtle rim from behind for silhouette separation"
- "Morning sun back rim at 5600K"
- "Crisp rim from overhead sodium lamps"
- "Back rim intensifies; grade warms"

## Lighting Transitions

Timestamp all lighting changes:
- "Light warms slightly" [at 0:07]
- "Lighting shifts to split key" [at 0:08]
- "Add faint rim from behind" [at 0:04]
- "Grade cools slightly" [at 0:09]

## Complete Lighting Example

```
[0:00-0:03] Soft 5600K key from camera right motivated by late-afternoon sun; 2:1 key/fill; thin haze for glow.

[0:03-0:07] Key maintains 5600K; add subtle back rim from horizon at 5800K; 2:1 ratio holds.

[0:07-0:10] Light warms to 5400K; grading shifts warmer with golden tones; 1.5:1 key/fill as sun diffuses.
```
