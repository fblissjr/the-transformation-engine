# Compound Transition: Dolly + Reflection Swap (Veo 3.1)

## Transition Type
**Advanced/Compound**: Camera Movement + Reflective Surface

## Description
Camera is positioned to show a reflection in water/mirror. Subject moves over/past the reflection, and as camera dollies/tilts to follow, we "enter" the reflection which becomes reality, transitioning to a different time/place.

## Use Cases
- Memory sequences
- Flashbacks
- Parallel timelines
- Complex narrative transitions
- Past/present connections

## Anatomy
1. **Scene A State** (0-3s): Establish reflection with subject approaching
2. **Transition Technique** (3-5s): Subject passes reflection, camera follows through it
3. **Scene B Reveal** (5-8s): Emerged from reflection into "reflection reality"
4. **Audio Bridge**: Watery ripple or glass tone during reflection crossing

## Technical Notes
- Advanced spatial concept: "reflection becomes reality"
- Test pattern as Veo 3.1 may interpret literally or creatively
- Works best with motivated camera movement
- Requires clear reflection establishment in Scene A

## Prompt Template

```
{{subject}} {{action_a}} down {{scene_a_location}}, {{scene_a_details}}. The camera is positioned low, reflecting {{subject}} and {{reflection_elements}} in {{reflection_surface}} between camera and subject. {{subject}} {{action_over_reflection}}, and as {{body_part}} passes over the {{reflection_description}}, the camera {{camera_movement}}, following {{subject_possessive}} stride. During this motion, the reflected image in the {{reflection_surface}} becomes the "real" image: we transition through the reflection into {{scene_b_context}} - {{scene_b_location}}, {{time_shift}}, {{scene_b_details}}.

Audio: {{audio_a}} transitions through a {{transition_audio_quality}} as camera dollies through {{reflection_surface}} reflection, emerging into {{audio_b}}.
```

## Variables
- `subject`: Detective, character, person
- `action_a`: Walks, runs, moves
- `scene_a_location`: Rain-soaked city street, lakeside path, mirrored hallway
- `scene_a_details`: Night, atmospheric details, lighting
- `reflection_elements`: Neon lights, streetlamps, environment details
- `reflection_surface`: Large puddle, lake surface, polished floor
- `action_over_reflection`: Steps over puddle, passes over reflection, walks across
- `body_part`: Foot, feet, shadow
- `reflection_description`: Reflection in puddle, mirrored image
- `camera_movement`: Tilts up and dollies forward, rises and pushes through, follows upward
- `subject_possessive`: Their, his, her
- `scene_b_context`: Memory, past version, alternate timeline
- `scene_b_location`: Same street but different time, different version of place
- `time_shift`: 10 years earlier, in the past, different era
- `scene_b_details`: How subject/environment changed
- `audio_a`: Starting audio environment
- `transition_audio_quality`: Watery ripple sound, glass tone, liquid resonance
- `audio_b`: New audio environment

## Example from Research

```
A detective walks down a rain-soaked city street at night, neon signs reflecting in puddles, approaching the camera. The camera is positioned low, reflecting the detective and neon lights in a large puddle between camera and subject. The detective steps over the puddle, and as their foot passes over the reflection, the camera tilts up and dollies forward rapidly, following the detective's stride. During this motion, the reflected image in the puddle becomes the "real" image: we transition through the reflection into a memory - the same street, but 10 years earlier, daytime, dry pavement, the detective younger, walking away from camera.

Audio: Rain on pavement and distant traffic transitions through a watery ripple sound as camera dollies through puddle reflection, emerging into quiet daytime street ambiance (birds, footsteps, distant conversations).
```

## Scene Extension Integration
**Best Match**: Transition method (complex narratives)
**Preservation**: Geographic location (same place, different time), subject identity
**User Prompt Keywords**: "through reflection", "reflection becomes reality", "into memory", "puddle transition"

## Red Flags to Avoid
- No clear reflection visible in Scene A
- Camera motion not motivated
- Reflection transition too abstract
- Missing watery/glassy audio transition

## Success Criteria
- Clear reflection established early
- Subject positioned relative to reflection
- Motivated camera movement (following subject)
- Moment when camera "enters" reflection clear
- Reflection-to-reality concept executed
- Time/context shift coherent
- Audio marks transition with liquid/glass tone
- Geographic continuity maintained
- Temporal shift clearly signaled
