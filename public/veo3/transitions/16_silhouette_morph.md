# Silhouette Morph Transition (Veo 3.1)

## Transition Type
**Creative**: Silhouette Transformation

## Description
Subject is backlit into silhouette. The silhouette shape is maintained but transforms into a different subject through overexposure or light manipulation. The shape provides continuity while identity changes.

## Use Cases
- Creative transformations
- Artist/subject connections
- Metaphorical transitions
- Identity or perspective shifts

## Anatomy
1. **Scene A State** (0-3s): Establish silhouette against bright background
2. **Transition Technique** (3-5s): Silhouette gesture, light intensifies, overexposure
3. **Scene B Reveal** (5-8s): New subject revealed matching silhouette shape
4. **Audio Bridge**: Profound silence or minimal audio during peak transition

## Prompt Template

```
{{shot_type}} of {{subject_a}} {{position}} at {{location_a}}, completely silhouetted against {{background_a}}, {{scene_a_details}}. The camera holds on this dramatic silhouette. {{subject_a}} {{gesture_action}}, creating {{new_silhouette_shape}}. As {{body_part}} {{gesture_completion}}, the {{background_a}} grows even more intense, flaring and overexposing the frame. When the overexposure fades, the silhouette is now different: it's {{subject_b}} against the same {{background_description}}. The camera {{camera_movement}} to reveal this is {{reveal_context}}, with the same color palette, and {{character_connection}}.

Audio: {{audio_a}} transition into profound silence during overexposure, then {{audio_b}} emerges.
```

## Variables
- `shot_type`: Backlit wide shot, silhouette shot, dramatic profile
- `subject_a`: Lone figure, person, character
- `position`: Standing at edge of cliff, positioned against sunset, backlit in doorway
- `location_a`: Cliff edge, hilltop, doorway, window
- `background_a`: Brilliant orange sky, bright light, sunset
- `scene_a_details`: Additional atmospheric details
- `gesture_action`: Raises arms slowly, extends wings, changes pose
- `new_silhouette_shape`: Arms extended creating wingspan, different shape profile
- `body_part`: Arms, hands, body
- `gesture_completion`: Reach full extension, complete motion, finalize pose
- `background_description`: Same or similar colored background
- `subject_b`: Tree with spreading branches, different shaped subject, artwork
- `camera_movement`: Pulls back, remains fixed, pushes in
- `reveal_context`: A painted tree on mural in gallery, photograph on wall, installation piece
- `character_connection`: Artist stands before it, viewer observes, connection revealed
- `audio_a`: Starting audio environment
- `audio_b`: New audio environment

## Example from Research

```
Backlit wide shot of a lone figure standing at the edge of a cliff at sunset, completely silhouetted against the brilliant orange sky, ocean visible far below. The camera holds on this dramatic silhouette. The figure raises their arms slowly, creating a new silhouette shape. As the arms reach full extension, the orange sunset light grows even more intense, flaring and overexposing the frame. When the overexposure fades, the silhouette is now different: it's a massive tree with spreading branches against the same orange sky. The camera pulls back to reveal this is a painted tree on a mural in an art gallery, with the same color palette, and a young artist stands before it, arms still extended, having just finished painting.

Audio: Ocean waves and wind at cliff edge transition into profound silence during overexposure, then quiet gallery ambiance (footsteps on hardwood, distant conversation, air conditioning) emerges.
```

## Scene Extension Integration
**Best Match**: Transition method
**Preservation**: Shape, silhouette, color palette, composition
**User Prompt Keywords**: "silhouette transforms", "backlit transition", "shape becomes", "silhouette morph"

## Red Flags to Avoid
- Silhouette not clearly defined
- No gesture or shape transformation
- Missing overexposure/light flare
- Weak connection between subjects

## Success Criteria
- Strong silhouette established
- High contrast backlit composition
- Clear shape maintained through transition
- Gesture creates anticipation
- Overexposure masks transformation
- New subject's shape echoes original
- Creative or metaphorical connection clear
- Silence enhances mystery during transition
