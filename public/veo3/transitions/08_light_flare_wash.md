# Light Flare Wash Transition (Veo 3.1)

## Transition Type
**Natural Element**: Overexposure Wash

## Description
Intense light source creates lens flare and overexposure that washes frame to white. Scene changes during white overexposure, then exposure normalizes to reveal new environment.

## Use Cases
- Trauma or medical sequences
- Spiritual or transcendent transitions
- Time jumps
- Dramatic tonal shifts (hopeful to harsh, or vice versa)

## Anatomy
1. **Scene A State** (0-3s): Establish scene with motivating light source
2. **Transition Technique** (3-5s): Light intensifies, overexposes to white
3. **Scene B Reveal** (5-8s): Exposure normalizes, new scene revealed
4. **Audio Bridge**: High-frequency tone during peak brightness, fading into new audio

## Prompt Template

```
{{scene_a_description}}. {{subject}} {{action_toward_light}} {{light_source}}. As {{pronoun}} does, the {{light_source}}'s light grows intensely bright, creating a massive lens flare that gradually overexposes the entire frame to white. In the brilliant white overexposure, the scene transitions. As exposure normalizes, we are now in {{scene_b_location}}, {{scene_b_description}}, {{lighting_continuity}}.

Audio: {{scene_a_audio}} are overwhelmed by a bright, high-frequency tone during overexposure, then fade into {{scene_b_audio}}.
```

## Variables
- `scene_a_description`: Starting scene setup
- `subject`: Character experiencing transition
- `action_toward_light`: Turns to face, looks directly at, walks toward
- `light_source`: Sun, headlights, explosion, lightning, hospital lights, etc.
- `pronoun`: They, she, he
- `scene_b_location`: New environment type
- `scene_b_description`: New scene details
- `lighting_continuity`: How lighting in Scene B relates to overexposure (same harsh light quality, softer now, etc.)
- `scene_a_audio`: Starting audio environment
- `scene_b_audio`: New audio environment

## Example from Research

```
A woman walks along a beach at golden hour, warm sunset light behind her, waves gently lapping at the shore. She turns to face the setting sun directly. As she does, the sun's light grows intensely bright, creating a massive lens flare that gradually overexposes the entire frame to white. In the brilliant white overexposure, the scene transitions. As exposure normalizes, we are now in a stark white hospital room, the woman lying in bed, harsh fluorescent lights overhead creating the same overexposed quality that now fades to normal exposure.

Audio: Beach sounds (waves, seagulls, wind) are overwhelmed by a bright, high-frequency tone during overexposure, then fade into quiet hospital ambiance (distant beeping, HVAC hum, muffled footsteps).
```

## Scene Extension Integration
**Best Match**: Transition method
**Preservation**: Subject continuity, light color/quality can be preserved (warm to warm, harsh to harsh)
**User Prompt Keywords**: "overexposed", "white wash", "bright flash", "light transition", "lens flare"

## Red Flags to Avoid
- No motivating light source
- Missing high-frequency audio tone
- Instant transition without gradual overexposure
- No exposure normalization phase in Scene B

## Success Criteria
- Clear light source motivation
- Gradual overexposure build
- Complete white frame at peak
- High-frequency tone during peak
- Smooth exposure recovery
- Thematic connection between scenes
