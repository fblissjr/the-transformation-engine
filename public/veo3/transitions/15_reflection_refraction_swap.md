# Reflection/Refraction Swap Transition (Veo 3.1)

## Transition Type
**Creative**: Mirror/Surface Reflection

## Description
Camera focuses on a reflection in a mirror, window, or water surface. As camera pushes toward or "through" the reflective surface, the reflection subtly shifts, and we transition from the real world into the reflection (or vice versa).

## Use Cases
- Inner/outer self exploration
- Time transitions (preparation to outcome)
- Parallel realities
- Subjective vs. objective perspectives

## Anatomy
1. **Scene A State** (0-3s): Establish reflection with real world visible
2. **Transition Technique** (3-5s): Camera pushes toward surface, reflection shifts
3. **Scene B Reveal** (5-8s): Camera "through" surface, now in reflection world
4. **Audio Bridge**: Glass resonance or water ripple during pass-through

## Prompt Template

```
{{camera_position}} of {{subject}} {{action_a}} in {{location_a}}, {{details_a}}. The camera is positioned {{perspective}}, showing {{subject_possessive}} {{reflection_description}}. Gradually, the camera pushes forward toward the {{reflective_surface}}. As the camera gets closer, the reflection begins to subtly change: {{reflection_changes}}. When the camera reaches the {{reflective_surface}} surface and "passes through" it, we are now in the reflection - {{new_perspective}} {{location_b}} where {{subject}} {{action_b}}, {{details_b}}.

Audio: {{audio_a}} transition with a subtle {{transition_audio}} as camera passes through {{reflective_surface}}, then {{audio_b}} takes over.
```

## Variables
- `camera_position`: Close-up, medium shot, over-shoulder
- `subject`: Character name or description
- `action_a`: What subject is doing initially
- `location_a`: Starting location (bathroom, car, window location)
- `details_a`: Scene A atmospheric details
- `perspective`: Behind her, from the side, at angle
- `subject_possessive`: Her, his, their
- `reflection_description`: Reflection in mirror, reflection in window
- `reflective_surface`: Mirror, window, puddle, glass, water
- `reflection_changes`: Details that shift in reflection
- `new_perspective`: Looking out from mirror into, viewing from reflection back at
- `location_b`: Where reflection "really" is
- `action_b`: What subject does in Scene B
- `details_b`: Scene B details
- `audio_a`: Starting audio with acoustic signature
- `transition_audio`: Glass resonance, water ripple, surface tone
- `audio_b`: New audio environment

## Example from Research

```
Close-up of a woman looking at herself in a bathroom mirror, dimly lit, applying makeup, morning routine sounds (water running, cosmetic containers clicking). The camera is positioned behind her, showing her reflection. Gradually, the camera pushes forward toward the mirror. As the camera gets closer, the reflection begins to subtly change: the woman's reflection starts wearing different clothes, the room behind her in the mirror shifts from bathroom to an office. When the camera reaches the mirror surface and "passes through" it, we are now in the reflection - looking out from the mirror into an office where the woman sits at a desk, looking toward us (the mirror position), makeup completed, ready for her workday.

Audio: Intimate bathroom acoustics (close, tiled surfaces) transition with a subtle glass resonance as camera passes through mirror, then office ambiance (keyboard clicks, muffled office chatter, HVAC) takes over.
```

## Scene Extension Integration
**Best Match**: Transition method
**Preservation**: Subject continuity, visual style can shift
**User Prompt Keywords**: "through mirror", "into reflection", "reflection becomes reality", "mirror transition"

## Red Flags to Avoid
- No clear reflective surface established
- Reflection changes too obviously or too fast
- Missing "pass through" moment
- No acoustic shift at surface crossing

## Success Criteria
- Reflective surface clearly visible
- Gradual camera push toward surface
- Subtle reflection shifts build anticipation
- Clear "pass through" moment
- Perspective flip is coherent
- Acoustic transition marks surface crossing
- Thematic connection between both sides
