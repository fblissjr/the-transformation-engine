# Cinematic Realism Scene Template (Veo 3.1)

## Scene Type: Cinematic Realism

### Characteristics
- **Framing**: Wide shots, aerial views, establishing shots
- **Camera**: Dynamic movement (tracking, orbiting, craning, dollying)
- **Audio Priority**: Environmental sounds, spatial audio, atmospheric presence
- **Word Count**: 150-250 words (concise, camera-focused)
- **Best For**: Landscapes, action sequences, vehicle scenes, establishing shots, aerial cinematography

## Optimal Structure

```
[Camera type + movement] [subject + action] [location/environment], [time of day/lighting].
[Camera movement details]. [Visual composition details]. [Action intensification or development].
[Audio cues focusing on spatial and environmental sounds].
```

## Camera Language (Critical)

### Primary Camera Types
- **Drone shot**: Aerial perspective, freedom of movement
- **Tracking shot**: Camera moves parallel to subject
- **Dolly shot**: Camera moves toward/away from subject (creates parallax)
- **Crane shot**: Camera ascends or descends
- **Steadicam**: Smooth handheld, flowing movement
- **Gimbal-stabilized**: Smooth electronic stabilization

### Camera Movements
- **Following**: Tracks behind or alongside moving subject
- **Orbiting**: Circles around subject (specify clockwise/counter-clockwise)
- **Ascending/Descending**: Vertical movement (craning up/down, drone rising/falling)
- **Pushing in/Pulling out**: Moving toward or away from subject
- **Panning**: Rotating left/right on fixed axis
- **Tilting**: Rotating up/down on fixed axis

### Movement Speed
- **Very slow**: Glacial, contemplative (building atmosphere)
- **Slow**: Gradual, smooth (establishing shots)
- **Medium**: Steady, purposeful (action following)
- **Fast**: Rapid, energetic (chase scenes, excitement)
- **Variable**: Changes speed for dramatic effect

## Lighting & Time of Day

### Golden Hour / Magic Hour
- Warm, golden light (sunrise/sunset)
- Long shadows, rich colors
- Soft, flattering illumination

### Midday / Harsh Light
- Bright, high-contrast
- Short shadows, vivid colors
- Clear visibility, intense atmosphere

### Blue Hour / Twilight
- Cool blue tones
- Soft ambient light
- Mysterious, transitional mood

### Night / Low Light
- Artificial light sources emphasized
- High contrast, dramatic shadows
- Cinematic noir atmosphere

## Example 1: Vehicle Chase (Desert Highway)

```
Aerial drone shot starting at car level tracking a sleek black sports car speeding along an empty desert highway at golden hour, dust trail streaming behind. Camera begins ascending while orbiting clockwise around the car, revealing vast landscape of red rock formations and endless horizon bathed in warm amber light. The car accelerates hard out of a curve, engine roaring aggressively, tires screaming on hot asphalt. Camera reaches apex 50 feet above, then descends rapidly back down, ending just behind and above the car as it races toward the sunset-filled horizon.

Engine sound: Deep, aggressive V8 growl modulating with distance - loud when close, quieter at apex. Tires screeching on asphalt during acceleration. Wind rushing past drone creating ambient whoosh. Distant echo of engine reverberating off canyon walls. Warm, atmospheric desert silence at height.
```

**Analysis**:
- Clear camera choreography (starts car-level, ascends, orbits, descends)
- Environmental scale emphasized (vast landscape, red rocks, endless horizon)
- Action beats (acceleration, curve exit)
- Spatial audio (modulating with camera distance)
- 117 words (concise, camera-focused)
- Golden hour lighting specified

## Example 2: Coastal Aerial Reveal

```
Drone shot slowly descending toward a coastal cliff edge at dawn, soft pink and orange light painting the sky. Camera starts high above ocean, waves crashing against rocks far below, seabirds circling in updrafts. As camera descends, a solitary lighthouse emerges into frame, white tower stark against dark cliffs, beacon rotating, casting sweeping beam across misty air. Drone continues descent, circling counter-clockwise around lighthouse, revealing small keeper's cottage attached at base, smoke rising from chimney. Camera ends at ground level, facing lighthouse door, first rays of sunlight striking the tower top.

Waves crashing rhythmically against rocks below, wind rushing past drone with salty ocean spray, seabirds calling distantly, lighthouse beacon mechanism humming mechanically, quiet crackling of fireplace from cottage chimney barely audible.
```

**Analysis**:
- Continuous descending motion with orbital element
- Gradual reveal technique (lighthouse emerges into frame)
- Dawn lighting progression (pink/orange sky, first rays striking tower)
- Layered environmental audio (waves, wind, birds, mechanical, fire)
- 129 words (concise, atmospheric)

## Example 3: Urban Tracking Shot

```
Steadicam tracking shot at street level following a cyclist weaving through busy city street at midday, bright sunlight creating harsh shadows between buildings. Camera starts behind cyclist, matching speed, then accelerates and moves alongside, capturing profile against blurred background of pedestrians and storefronts. Cyclist suddenly veers right into narrow alley, camera whip-pans to follow, temporarily losing subject in motion blur, then refocuses tracking cyclist from front as they emerge from alley shadow into bright plaza beyond. Camera pulls back while ascending slightly, revealing full plaza with fountain centerpiece.

Bicycle chain clicking rhythmically, tires humming on pavement, city ambiance loud (car horns, conversations, footsteps), sound briefly echoing and dampening in alley, then opening up with fountain splashing and plaza acoustics as space widens.
```

**Analysis**:
- Ground-level energy (street perspective)
- Camera position changes (behind, alongside, front)
- Whip pan technique incorporated
- Audio reflects spatial changes (open street, narrow alley, plaza)
- Midday harsh lighting specified
- 115 words (action-focused)

## Template with Variables

```
{{camera_type}} {{camera_movement}} {{subject}} {{action}} {{location}}, {{time_of_day}}. {{camera_continues}} {{visual_reveals}} {{environment_details}}. {{action_development}} {{sensory_details}}.

{{audio_primary}} {{audio_secondary}} {{audio_ambient}} {{spatial_audio_notes}}.
```

### Variable Guide
- `camera_type`: Drone, tracking, dolly, crane, aerial, steadicam
- `camera_movement`: Following, orbiting, ascending, descending, tracking
- `subject`: Car, person, boat, animal, object
- `action`: Speeding, running, flying, moving, traveling
- `location`: Desert highway, coastal cliff, city street, mountain pass
- `time_of_day`: Golden hour, dawn, midday, twilight, night
- `camera_continues`: Next phase of camera choreography
- `visual_reveals`: What camera movement reveals or emphasizes
- `environment_details`: Scale, geography, atmospheric conditions
- `action_development`: How action progresses or intensifies
- `audio_primary`: Main sound directly from subject/action
- `audio_secondary`: Environmental interactions
- `audio_ambient`: Background atmosphere
- `spatial_audio_notes`: How sound changes with camera distance/position

## Camera Movement Patterns for Common Scenarios

### Reveal Pattern
```
Start: Close/obscured → Move: Pull back or ascend → End: Wide/revealed
```
**Use**: Establishing shots, dramatic reveals, scale demonstration

### Pursuit Pattern
```
Start: Behind subject → Move: Track alongside → End: In front or orbiting
```
**Use**: Vehicle chases, running sequences, energetic action

### Intimate to Epic Pattern
```
Start: Close detail → Move: Ascend and pull back → End: Massive wide shot
```
**Use**: Character to environment connection, lonely vastness, scale contrast

### Orbit Showcase Pattern
```
Start: Fixed angle → Move: 180° or 360° orbit → End: Opposite or starting angle
```
**Use**: Product shots, character introductions, location showcasing

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### Camera Mistakes
- ❌ Vague movement ("camera moves")
- ❌ No movement speed specified
- ❌ Unclear camera position (where is camera physically?)
- ❌ No spatial progression described
- ✅ Specific movement with speed and clear choreography

### Environment Mistakes
- ❌ Generic location description
- ❌ No time of day/lighting specified
- ❌ Missing scale indicators
- ❌ No atmospheric details
- ✅ Rich environmental context with lighting and scale

### Audio Mistakes
- ❌ Ignoring spatial audio (near/far, echoing, dampened)
- ❌ Only describing subject sounds
- ❌ Missing environmental ambiance
- ✅ Layered audio with spatial awareness

## Checklist for Cinematic Scenes

- [ ] Camera type specified (drone, tracking, dolly, crane)
- [ ] Camera movement detailed (following, orbiting, ascending, speed)
- [ ] Time of day and lighting described
- [ ] Environment scale and geography clear
- [ ] Subject action dynamic and visual
- [ ] Camera choreography has clear beginning, middle, end
- [ ] Audio layered (primary, secondary, ambient)
- [ ] Spatial audio noted (distance, echo, perspective)
- [ ] Word count 150-250 (concise)
- [ ] Visual composition emphasized

## When to Use This Template

### Perfect For:
- Establishing shots
- Vehicle scenes (cars, motorcycles, boats, planes)
- Landscape cinematography
- Aerial/drone shots
- Chase sequences
- Outdoor action
- Scale demonstrations

## Advanced Techniques

### Compound Movements
Combine movements for dynamic shots:
```
"Drone descends while orbiting clockwise"
"Tracking shot that accelerates and transitions to whip pan"
"Crane ascends while panning left across skyline"
```

### Perspective Shifts
Change camera relationship to subject:
```
"Starts behind subject, moves alongside, ends in front"
"Ground level ascending to bird's eye view"
"Close tracking expanding to wide aerial"
```

### Timing Transitions
Align camera movement with action beats:
```
"Camera push-in as car accelerates"
"Ascent begins as subject reaches peak of jump"
"Orbit speed matches rotation of subject"
```
